May I Help You

'Not doing great things, but little things with great love!'

TAMMANA is a Punjab Registered, Chandigarh Based, No Profit NGO working without any financial aid - passionately since 16+ years on a very consistent basis for the welfare of the society. Its team of over 50+ like minded members enthusiasts from various fields & backgrounds, who work selflessly & voluntarily without a salary or a fee, is founded and led by Isha Kakaria, who is also a State Awardee.

KEY POINTS OF THE ORGANIZATION

  • Registered in Punjab
  • Operates primarily in Chandigarh/Mohali
  • Working consistently since 16+ years ( Founded on 1st September 2008 )
  • Founded & led by Isha Kakaria.
  • Team is 50+ members/like minded enthusiasts from different fields and backgrounds. Mostly working.
  • Organises Monthly events. Mostly on weekends.
  • Membership with minimum age = 21, with no maximum bar
  • Not financially supported or granted by the Government
  • Tammana is completely voluntary
  • No Salary/Fee for Membership/Certification
  • The Team contributes in cash or in kind for each monthly event/case we do
  • Provides Certified Internship, age minimum 18 years.
  • Awarded by the Government & many reputed platforms
  • Annual Award Ceremony to recognize best contributors of the Team
  • Takes pride in being disciplined, transparent & having an open culture.
  • Visit 'what we do' page for more.
